# Entrepreneurship — MagMount and Case Analysis

**Introduction to Entrepreneurship** · Daniels College of Business · Spring 2025

The course ran from opportunity identification through business model design, market validation, financial planning, and an investor pitch. Most of the term went into developing an original venture concept; the rest applied the same frameworks to real companies.

## MagMount

MagMount is a compact magnetic ski mount that attaches to any vehicle door panel, giving skiers a safe, hands-free place to rest their skis while changing boots or sorting gear, at a considerably lower price than existing products like SnoStrip. The idea came from personal experience in resort parking lots and was developed through a Business Model Canvas, secondary research on the ski equipment market and resort traffic, competitor pricing analysis, a promotional strategy and elevator pitch, a cost and pricing budget, and a full investor pitch deck.

## Case work

**Airbnb and product-market fit.** An analysis of how Airbnb reached product-market fit through Y Combinator's "do things that don't scale" approach, tracing decisions like in-person host photography, direct customer feedback loops, and the Obama O's cereal campaign as evidence of iterative refinement.

**Founder interview — MT Provisions.** A profile of the founder of MT Provisions, a Colorado ultralight backcountry gear company, following how a frustration on the trail became a validated product through field testing and customer co-development.

**Tools and methods:** Business Model Canvas · secondary market research · competitive analysis · financial budgeting · pitch development
