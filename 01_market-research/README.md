# Market Research — Consumer Perception of DU's 4D Experience

**MKTG 2930: Methods of Marketing Research** · Daniels College of Business · Spring 2025

A full-cycle primary research study on how University of Denver undergraduates perceive the 4D Experience, DU's signature student-development program, and where the university could position it more effectively. The project ran the complete research process: framing the problem, defining target segments from secondary data, conducting qualitative interviews, designing and validating a Qualtrics survey, cleaning the response data, and analyzing it in Excel before presenting findings and recommendations.

This was a team project. The work in this folder covers the research design, the interview protocol and transcripts, the questionnaire and codebook, the cleaned survey data, and the final report.

## What the project involved

The research statement and segmentation rationale came first, grounded in demographic, psychographic, and behavioral criteria from secondary sources. Six in-depth interviews across different student profiles shaped the questionnaire, which was deployed to a stratified sample of DU undergraduates through Qualtrics. Raw responses were coded, cleaned, and analyzed with frequencies, distributions, and cross-tabulations, and the results were translated into positioning recommendations for the university.

**Tools and methods:** Qualtrics · Excel · survey design and validation · sampling · qualitative interviewing · descriptive and cross-tabular analysis · Hoovers and Mintel for secondary data
