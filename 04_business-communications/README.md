# Food Bank of the Rockies — Activating Workplace Giving

Final live-case project for Business Communications at the Daniels College of Business, University of Denver (Spring 2026).

## The project

Food Bank of the Rockies (FBR) is the largest-service-area food bank in the United States, serving all of Colorado and Wyoming and distributing over 100 million pounds of food a year — the equivalent of 200,000 meals every day. It runs on 23,000+ annual volunteers (roughly 68 full-time-equivalents) and a newly expanded facility that doubled its capacity.

Workplace giving accounts for only about $1.5M of FBR's ~$31M philanthropic budget — under 5%, with no dedicated owner. This project makes the case that the gap is **activation, not enrollment**: FBR is already on Benevity, YourCause, and Bonterra but isn't working them.

**The recommendation:** give workplace giving a dedicated part-time lead focused on lead generation — signing employers with volunteer-grant ("Dollars for Doers") and matching-gift programs. Capturing even 1 in 4 of FBR's 140,000 annual volunteer hours at ~$10/hr is $350K+ a year — about a million meals — **without asking a single volunteer for a cent.**
