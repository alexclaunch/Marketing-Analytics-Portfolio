**Instructor:** Dr. Aaron Duncan · **Credits:** 4 · **Term:** Spring 2025

**Project: Consumer Perception of DU's 4D Experience**

A full-cycle research study conducted for the University of Denver, examining how undergraduate students perceive DU's signature 4D Experience program and identifying opportunities for stronger marketing and positioning.

- Identified and justified target market segments using demographic, psychographic, geographic, and behavioral criteria supported by secondary data
- Developed a structured interview protocol and conducted six in-depth qualitative interviews across diverse student profiles
- Designed a Qualtrics survey instrument with validated reliability; deployed to a stratified sample of DU undergraduates
- Built a codebook and cleaned raw Qualtrics response data for analysis
- Conducted univariate analysis (frequencies, distributions) and multivariate analysis (cross-tabulations) in Excel
- Delivered strategic findings and recommendations in a final group presentation

**Tools & methods:** Qualtrics · Excel · Survey design & validation · Sampling methodology · Qualitative interviewing · Descriptive & inferential statistics · Hoovers · Mintel

### 📈 02 — Business Analytics (INFO 2020: Analytics III — Business Modeling & Analysis)
**Instructor:** Dr. Holly Roof · **Credits:** 4 · **Term:** Winter 2025

This course introduced multiple regression analysis, time-series forecasting, and Monte Carlo simulation for solving applied business problems. Applications spanned economic forecasting, supply chain management, and financial planning. Projects emphasized Excel modeling alongside formal written and oral presentation skills.

---

**Project 2 — Superior Building Supply: Lost Sales Managerial Report**

Superior Building Supply suffered fire damage and disputed its lost sales with its insurer. Using 48 months of pre-fire historical data, I estimated what sales would have been during the four-month closure and assessed whether the store was entitled to excess-revenue compensation given a post-fire construction boom driven by $8 billion in relief funds.

- Built a 4-point moving average model and Naïve forecasting model
- Constructed a multiple regression model with seasonal adjustment for the closure period
- Evaluated model accuracy using MAD, MSE, and MAPE
- Analyzed whether the county-wide revenue spike constituted compensable excess sales
- Delivered findings in a formal managerial report with executive summary and quantified recommendations

**Project 1 — University Admissions Regression Analysis**

Built a multiple regression model to identify which applicant variables — GPA, test scores, letter of recommendation strength, and university ranking — significantly predict admission probability for prospective students.

- Variable selection and collinearity assessment
- Regression output interpretation (coefficients, p-values, R²)
- Written executive summary translating statistical findings into actionable admissions insights

**Project 3 — Franklin Mintage Retirement Portfolio Simulation**

Designed a Monte Carlo simulation in Excel to model a 20-year retirement savings trajectory under market variability, and identify the investment rate required to hit a $1,000,000 goal.

- Modeled salary growth with a uniform distribution (0.2%–5.1%) and portfolio returns with a normal distribution (mean: 9.95%, SD: 4.8%)
- Used Goal Seek to determine required investment rate (8.65%) to hit the $1M target
- Ran simulation to find 18.5% probability of reaching goal in 20 years vs. 99.1% at 25 years
- Delivered findings with scenario comparisons and concrete savings recommendations

**Tools & methods:** Excel · Multiple linear regression · Time-series forecasting · Monte Carlo simulation · Goal Seek · MAD / MSE / MAPE · Business report writing

### 💡 03 — Entrepreneurship (Intro to Entrepreneurship)
**Instructor:** Professor McCaffery · **Term:** Spring 2025

This course covered the entrepreneurial process from opportunity identification through business model development, market validation, financial planning, and investor pitching. Projects applied real frameworks — Business Model Canvas, product-market fit analysis, competitive landscaping — to both original venture concepts and real-world case studies.

---

**MagMount — Original Startup Concept**

Developed a full venture concept for MagMount, a compact magnetic ski mount that attaches to any vehicle door panel, giving skiers a safe, hands-free place to rest skis during boot changes and gear transitions — at a significantly lower price point than existing competitors like SnoStrip.

- Identified the unmet market need through personal experience and competitive gap analysis
- Built a Business Model Canvas mapping value proposition, customer segments, revenue streams, and key activities
- Conducted secondary market research on the ski equipment industry, resort traffic trends, and competitor pricing
- Wrote a promotional strategy and elevator pitch
- Built a financial budget with cost projections and pricing model
- Delivered a full investor pitch deck

**AirBnB Case Study — Product-Market Fit Analysis**

Analyzed how Airbnb achieved product-market fit through Y Combinator's "do things that don't scale" philosophy — tracing decisions like in-person host photography, real-time customer feedback loops, and the Obama O's cereal campaign as evidence of founder resilience and iterative product refinement.

**Entrepreneurship Interview — MT Provisions**

Conducted and wrote a profile of Gabe Allen, founder of MT Provisions, a Colorado-based ultralight backcountry gear company. The paper traces how a trail frustration — no stable cooking surface on backcountry trips — became a validated product through iterative field testing and customer co-development.
