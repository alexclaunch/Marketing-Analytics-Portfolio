# Business Analytics — Regression, Forecasting, and Simulation

**INFO 2020: Analytics III — Business Modeling and Analysis** · Daniels College of Business · Winter 2025

Three applied modeling projects built in Excel, each paired with a written report that translates the statistics into a business recommendation. The course covered multiple regression, time-series forecasting, and Monte Carlo simulation, and every project was graded on the quality of the writing as much as the model.

## The projects

**Superior Building Supply — lost sales after a fire.** A building supply store closed for four months after fire damage and disputed its lost-sales claim with its insurer. Using 48 months of pre-fire sales, I built a four-point moving average, a naïve forecast, and a seasonally adjusted multiple regression to estimate what sales would have been during the closure, compared the models on MAD, MSE, and MAPE, and assessed whether a county-wide construction boom driven by $8 billion in relief funding entitled the store to excess-revenue compensation. The findings went into a formal managerial report with an executive summary and quantified recommendations.

**University admissions — what predicts acceptance.** A multiple regression on applicant data to identify which variables (GPA, test scores, recommendation strength, university ranking) significantly predict admission, with attention to variable selection and collinearity. The executive summary turns coefficient and p-value interpretation into plain-language guidance for an admissions office.

**Retirement portfolio — Monte Carlo simulation.** A 20-year retirement savings model under market uncertainty, with salary growth drawn from a uniform distribution (0.2% to 5.1%) and returns from a normal distribution (mean 9.95%, SD 4.8%). Goal Seek identified the 8.65% investment rate needed to reach a $1M target, and the simulation showed an 18.5% probability of hitting it in 20 years versus 99.1% in 25. The write-up compares scenarios and gives concrete savings recommendations. This project was completed with a partner.

**Tools and methods:** Excel · multiple linear regression · time-series forecasting · Monte Carlo simulation · Goal Seek · MAD / MSE / MAPE · managerial report writing
